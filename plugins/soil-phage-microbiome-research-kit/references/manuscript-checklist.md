# Manuscript Checklist

Use this checklist for soil microbiome, phage, virome, metagenomics, and microbial ecology manuscripts.

## Scientific framing

- Research question is explicit.
- Hypotheses are stated before results interpretation.
- Sampling design supports the claims.
- Treatment, site, time, batch, and environmental covariates are not confounded or are handled transparently.
- Negative controls, positive controls, and contamination checks are described when relevant.

## Methods reproducibility

- Raw data accession or storage plan is described.
- Metadata fields and sample exclusions are documented.
- Software names, versions, parameters, database names, database paths, and database release dates are reported when available.
- Statistical models include formulas, distance metrics, permutation design, correction method, and effect sizes.
- No version number, database date, citation, or result is invented.

## Results and discussion

- Results are separated from interpretation.
- Claims include effect size, uncertainty, and ecological meaning, not only p-values.
- Figures and tables are referenced in logical order.
- Soil ecology interpretation is tied to measured variables and study design.
- Limitations are explicit.

## Submission

- Figures are exported as journal-ready PDF, SVG, TIFF, or PNG as required.
- Supplementary tables are complete and regenerable from scripts.
- Data availability, code availability, author contributions, funding, conflicts, and acknowledgements are complete.
