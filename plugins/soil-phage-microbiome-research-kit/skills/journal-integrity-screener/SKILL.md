# Journal Integrity Screener

Use this skill when screening a journal for submission suitability, reliability, indexing, transparency, warning list status, or use as a source of core evidence.

## Safety rules

- Do not fabricate impact factor, JCR quartile, CiteScore, APC, review time, indexing, warning list status, ethics policy, or peer review claims.
- All current status information must be verified online or supplied by the user.
- CAS warning list status is a risk signal, not an automatic misconduct judgment.
- Do not declare a journal predatory from one indicator alone.
- Mark unverifiable items as `[requires manual verification]`.

## Sources to check

- Chinese Academy of Sciences / CAS International Journal Early Warning List when relevant.
- Think. Check. Submit. checklist.
- DOAJ for open-access journal indexing.
- COPE membership or publication ethics policy where relevant.
- Publisher website.
- Journal editorial board.
- Indexing databases such as Web of Science, Scopus, PubMed, and DOAJ when relevant.
- APC and waiver policy.
- Peer review policy.
- Contact information transparency.

## Screening fields

Check:

- Journal title.
- ISSN and eISSN.
- Publisher.
- Official website.
- Indexing status.
- Warning list status.
- DOAJ status.
- Peer review policy.
- APC transparency.
- Editorial board transparency.
- Scope fit.
- Suspicious title similarity or hijacked journal risk.
- Unusually fast review or guaranteed acceptance claims.
- Aggressive solicitation red flags.

## Risk levels

Use:

- Low risk.
- Moderate risk.
- High risk.
- Avoid for core evidence.
- Requires manual verification.

## Output format

When triggered, provide:

- Journal screening table.
- Source URLs and date verified.
- Risk level and rationale.
- Missing verification list.
- Recommendation for submission or citation use.
