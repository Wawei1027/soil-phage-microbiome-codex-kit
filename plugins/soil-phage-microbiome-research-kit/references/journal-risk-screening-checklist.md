# Journal Risk Screening Checklist

## CAS early warning list

- Check CAS International Journal Early Warning List when relevant.
- Treat warning status as a risk flag, not a misconduct judgment.

## Think Check Submit

- Apply journal identity, peer review, editorial board, fees, and publisher transparency questions.

## DOAJ

- Check whether an open-access journal is listed in DOAJ when relevant.

## COPE / publication ethics policy

- Check COPE membership or the journal's publication ethics policy.
- Record source URL and date verified.

## Indexing

- Verify indexing claims through Web of Science, Scopus, PubMed, DOAJ, or other relevant databases.

## APC transparency

- Check whether APC amount, waiver policy, and payment timing are clear.

## Editorial board

- Check editor names, affiliations, and transparency.

## Peer review process

- Check whether peer review type and process are stated.

## Hijacked journal risk

- Confirm official website, ISSN, publisher, and title.
- Watch for suspiciously similar titles or unofficial websites.

## Aggressive solicitation risk

- Treat unsolicited promises, guaranteed acceptance, and unusually fast review claims as risk signals.
