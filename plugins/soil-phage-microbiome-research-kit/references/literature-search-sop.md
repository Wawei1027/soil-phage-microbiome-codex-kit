# Literature Search SOP

## Define research question

- State the main research question.
- Define population, system, data type, and process.
- Decide whether the goal is background, method support, direct evidence, caveat, or proposal framing.

## Generate keywords

- Break the question into concepts.
- Add synonyms and related terms.
- Combine soil microbiome, phage, virome, metagenomics, amplicon, microbial ecology, biogeochemistry, carbon cycling, nitrogen cycling, phosphorus cycling, disturbance, land use, and environmental gradients when relevant.

## Choose databases

- Google Scholar.
- Web of Science or Scopus if available.
- PubMed if relevant.
- Crossref.
- OpenAlex.
- Semantic Scholar.
- Publisher pages.

## Seed paper strategy

- Start with verified seed papers.
- Record why each seed paper is relevant.
- Use seed papers to identify recurring authors, labs, methods, and concepts.

## Backward citation tracking

- Review key references cited by seed papers.
- Verify metadata before adding to the evidence matrix.

## Forward citation tracking

- Identify newer papers that cite seed papers.
- Prioritize recent papers with direct relevance and verifiable metadata.

## Inclusion/exclusion criteria

- Define topic, system, data type, methods, publication type, and date range.
- Exclude unverifiable records from core evidence until verified.

## Verification before citation

- Check DOI and citation metadata.
- Check journal risk when needed.
- Check paper integrity risk when feasible.
- Record verification status, source URL, and date verified.
