# Paper Integrity Screening Checklist

## DOI verification

- Confirm DOI resolves.
- Confirm title, authors, journal, year, volume, issue, and pages or article number.

## Crossref/Crossmark

- Check Crossref metadata.
- Check Crossmark updates when available.

## Retraction Watch

- Check for retraction records when accessible.

## PubMed/publisher retraction notices

- Check PubMed for biomedical papers.
- Check publisher correction, retraction, and expression of concern pages.

## PubPeer

- Check PubPeer if available.
- Record concerns without making independent misconduct accusations.

## Correction/expression of concern

- Record correction status.
- Record expression of concern status.
- Describe what the notice says if verified.

## Data/image concern if public

- Record publicly documented image, data, or paper mill concerns only when source-backed.

## Citation use recommendation

- Safe for background.
- Use cautiously.
- Avoid as core evidence.
- Do not cite unless discussing retraction/problem.
