# Evidence Synthesis Matrix

Use this skill to convert verified literature into a structured evidence matrix for writing, review, proposal, manuscript revision, or journal selection.

## Safety rules

- Do not use unverified papers to support key claims.
- Prefer verified core evidence in Introduction, Discussion, and review writing.
- Do not use risky papers as core evidence.
- Mark uncertain entries as `[unverified]` or `[requires manual verification]`.
- Do not fabricate paper conclusions, methods, sample sizes, journal risk status, or integrity status.

## Inputs

Accept:

- Verified papers.
- DOI list.
- BibTeX or RIS.
- Paper summaries.
- Screening matrix.
- User-provided PDFs or abstracts.

## Evidence matrix fields

Include:

- citation_key.
- DOI.
- title.
- year.
- journal.
- publisher.
- study system.
- research question.
- data type.
- methods.
- main finding.
- relevance to my question.
- evidence type.
- sample size / scale.
- limitations.
- citation role.
- journal risk status.
- paper integrity status.
- verification status.
- use recommendation.

## Citation role categories

Use:

- Background.
- Method support.
- Direct evidence.
- Contrasting evidence.
- Limitation/caveat.
- Not recommended.

## Risk labels

Use:

- Verified core evidence.
- Useful but limited.
- Use with caution.
- Avoid as core evidence.
- Exclude until verified.

## Output format

When triggered, provide:

- Evidence matrix.
- Claim-to-evidence map.
- Recommended citation roles.
- Papers excluded or requiring verification.
- Writing notes for Introduction or Discussion.
