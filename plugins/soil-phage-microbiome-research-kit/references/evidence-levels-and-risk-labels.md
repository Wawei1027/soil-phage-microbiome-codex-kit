# Evidence Levels And Risk Labels

## Verified core evidence

Use when metadata is verified, the paper is directly relevant, the journal risk is acceptable, and no known integrity issue affects the claim being cited.

## Useful but limited

Use when the paper is relevant but has limitations such as small scale, indirect system match, older methods, or limited scope.

## Use with caution

Use when the paper has partial verification, journal risk flags, correction notices, methodological concerns, or weak relevance.

## Avoid as core evidence

Use when the paper or journal has high risk flags, unresolved concerns, or weak verification. It may be mentioned only with careful caveats.

## Exclude until verified

Use when metadata, DOI, source, or publication status cannot be verified.

## Retracted / do not cite as positive evidence

Use when a paper is retracted. Do not cite it as positive evidence unless discussing the retraction or research integrity issue itself.
